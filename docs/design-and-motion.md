# Diskoshaman TV design and motion specification

Design in the working site around the client's confirmed mix of clean navigation, bold cinematic motion and an elegant studio feel. Use the existing showreel and brand cues, with a hero entrance, a featured-project scroll sequence, page transitions and menu and hover interactions. See the [client decisions](PRD.md#client-decisions).

## Art direction

Apple's navigation informs the compact header and simple type hierarchy. The requested Bugatti reference informs the room between the outer controls and centre mark. [Apple](https://www.apple.com/), [Bugatti](https://www.bugatti.com/)

Calls to action share one pill button (`PillLink`). A highlight orbits its rim and a reflection crosses the face while motion runs. Primary buttons (Book a call, Our Work, Meet the studio and interior actions) use gold glass with a gold arrow disc; on hover or keyboard focus a gold fill wipes in from the left and the arrow turns. The secondary button (Contact Us) uses smoked glass with a silver rim; champagne glass rises from below and the arrow slides forward. In a very narrow phone header the booking arrow drops out so the label stays whole.

The homepage uses a short introduction and a clear first action. Focus TV's portfolio informs the use of distinct imagery for each project. Give the Diskoshaman layout its own composition using the studio's imagery. [Apple](https://www.apple.com/), [Focus TV production portfolio](https://focustv.tv/)

Use the current brand colours as the base and add an accent where it supports hierarchy or contrast. Choose readable fonts with a consistent hierarchy. Use distinct stills for different projects and test contrast against the lightest and darkest frames behind text. Make the booking action prominent and keep enquiry available.

The primary colour is the studio's original gold (`#CC9828`). Use a brighter gold for fine print over film and a darker ochre on white. A smoked-glass curve frames the emblem over the opening film. On scroll, the surrounding glass follows the visitor's theme and dips around the emblem. Body and footer surfaces follow the same theme. [Studio brand and original reel](https://diskoshaman.tv/)

The approved opening and studio story set the reference for the redesign. Use warm light against blackened metal, muted brass, and the colour already present in each film. Big Shoulders carries the display titles and Manrope carries reading and controls. Keep one dominant film or photograph in each opening view. Portfolio labels move below the frame in the archive and sit on a protected dark area in the screenings.

The screenings and photographed set pieces use their authored film lighting in both themes. The archive, reading areas, enquiry form and footer adopt the visitor's theme.

Podium's substantial film frames and measured transitions informed the tempo of the featured work. Chems.Studio's separation of film discovery from project context informed the expandable archive and quieter controls. Focus TV's distinct project imagery informed the featured selection. The two angled side prints, production lens and curtain-lit portrait are composed around Diskoshaman's own stills. [Podium case study](https://tympanus.net/codrops/2026/06/23/podium-building-a-website-where-running-becomes-storytelling/), [Chems.Studio case study](https://tympanus.net/codrops/2026/08/08/designing-a-flexible-digital-archive-for-chems-studios-creative-practice/), [Focus TV](https://focustv.tv/)

The corporate service section offers two preview treatments using studio material. The Video excerpt is a six-second, silent production excerpt from the existing reel. Film frames rotates through the corporate project stills. The other four services use their own frame sequences in preview. The clip and frame sequences run near the viewport and provide a pause control. Review the two treatments in the working site before selecting the release treatment.

Place Brands & collaborators immediately after the studio story and before Selected work. Present the 16 published marks as slides in a rotating projector magazine, with bevelled metal frames and ivory or smoked mattes matched to the original colours. Set the heading and controls lower beside the magazine. Use a graphite-graded still from the studio's own reel and a fine viewfinder edge to frame the set. The 35mm case study informs turning a physical film object with scroll. The Jason Bergh case study informs the use of footage as background texture. [Diskoshaman TV homepage](https://diskoshaman.tv/), [35mm case study](https://tympanus.net/codrops/2025/05/30/deconstructing-the-35mm-website-a-look-at-the-process-and-technical-details/), [Jason Bergh case study](https://tympanus.net/codrops/2026/02/20/cinematic-presence-the-directors-cut-of-the-jason-bergh-experience/)

## Mobile and media

- Reserve the full glass curve in the mobile header grid. Keep at least 8 px clear before the booking button and respect the right safe area. Show the full Book a call label and add its arrow when the side column has room.
- Keep the desktop story order and interaction intent at phone widths. Use smaller video variants, art-directed stills and a shorter scroll travel to fit the viewport.
- Reserve space for all media with a set aspect ratio. Keep the poster visible until the hero video renders a frame. Play any automatic preview muted and inline, with a visible pause control. Provide Play when autoplay is unavailable.
- Maintain legible text when mobile browser chrome changes height. Check orientation change, swipes, back navigation, layout reflow and long titles.
- Use static hero, Work and service media states when reduced motion is enabled. Provide a still-first path if media is unavailable or expensive to load.
- Aim for WCAG 2.2 AA across the delivered interface. Use semantic controls, visible focus, labelled fields and appropriate text alternatives and captions. [WCAG 2.2](https://www.w3.org/TR/WCAG22/)
